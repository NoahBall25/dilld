How the Monthly Mystery Can Drop works (and how to never miss one)

By The DILL'D Lab · October 21, 2026 · 4 min read

On this page

How it works This month's drop: Wasabi Brine Fizz How to get in first Quick FAQ

Once a month, we brew one flavour that's too weird for the core lineup. We make a fixed number of cases. When they're gone, the flavour goes into the vault and never comes back. That's the Mystery Can Drop.

How it works

  1. The countdown starts. The timer on the homepage drop section shows exactly when the current drop closes.
  2. VIPs get the link first. People on the drop list get a private purchase link two hours before we post anywhere else.
  3. Public sale. Everyone else gets access when we post on TikTok and Instagram.
  4. The vault. When the last case sells, the flavour is retired. No re-runs.

This month's drop: Wasabi Brine Fizz

Drop 04 is Wasabi Brine Fizz: a sharp horseradish heat over our Classic Dill base. It's $4.50 a can and limited to 5,000 cases worldwide.

How to get in first

Join the drop list from the Mystery Can section or the newsletter box in the footer of any page. You'll get:

  • The secret purchase link two hours early
  • An optional SMS alert the second a drop opens
  • Restock alerts for the core flavours

We don't spam. One message when a drop opens, one when it's about to close.

Quick FAQ

Can I order more than one case?

Each customer can order up to two 12-packs per drop so more people get a shot.

Where do drops ship?

Canada, the US, the UK, the EU, Australia and Japan, the same as our core flavours.

Will a vaulted flavour ever come back?

No. That's the deal. If one gets enough noise it might inspire a new flavour, but the original stays in the vault.
