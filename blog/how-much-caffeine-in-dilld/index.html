How much caffeine is in a can of DILL'D?

By The DILL'D Lab · November 4, 2026 · 5 min read

On this page

The number: 180mg per can Why exactly 180mg? How many cans is too many? Does the pickle brine change anything? How to drink it like a pro

Short answer: 180mg per 355ml can. That's a big number on purpose, and it's also the most caffeine a single can of energy drink is allowed to carry in Canada. Here's how that stacks up, and how to enjoy it without regretting it.

The number: 180mg per can

Every DILL'D flavour, from Classic Dill to Dill Candy, contains 180mg of caffeine in a 12 fl oz (355ml) can. We don't do "light" versions and we don't hide the number in tiny print. It's on the front of the can and on our Brine Specs panel.

For context, a regular brewed coffee usually sits somewhere between 80mg and 140mg per cup depending on the beans and the brew, and a shot of espresso is roughly 60–75mg. One DILL'D lands a little above a strong mug of coffee.

Why exactly 180mg?

Health Canada regulates caffeinated energy drinks as food and caps them at 180mg of caffeine per single-serve container. We formulated right at that line: the most energy a can can legally carry here, with nothing sneaky on top.

Because of that, every can also has to carry a caution statement. Ours reads: not recommended for children, pregnant or breastfeeding women, or people sensitive to caffeine. You'll find the same notice at the bottom of every page on this site.

How many cans is too many?

Health Canada's general guidance for healthy adults is a maximum of about 400mg of caffeine a day from all sources, which includes coffee, tea, chocolate and pre-workout. For people who are pregnant, planning to be, or breastfeeding, the guidance drops to 300mg a day. Teens and kids have much lower limits based on body weight.

  • One can = 180mg, roughly 45% of an adult's daily max.
  • Two cans = 360mg, which leaves almost no room for that morning coffee.
  • Our rule: one DILL'D, enjoyed, is the move. It's a treat, not a hydration plan.

Does the pickle brine change anything?

The brine brings the flavour and about 460mg of sodium per can. That salty hit is the whole personality of the drink, but it doesn't change how caffeine works in your body. If you're watching your sodium, count a DILL'D the way you'd count a bag of chips.

How to drink it like a pro

  1. Drink it cold. Brine tastes sharper and cleaner below 4°C.
  2. Sip it. 180mg hits faster than you'd think, especially on an empty stomach.
  3. Skip it after mid-afternoon if you'd like to sleep tonight.
  4. Never mix it with alcohol.

⚡ Bottom line: 180mg per can, the legal max in Canada, built for one-can moments. Respect the dill.
