Every DILL'D flavour, ranked from mild to unhinged

By The DILL'D Lab · October 28, 2026 · 6 min read

On this page

A pickle energy drink sounds like a dare, and honestly, it kind of is. But every DILL'D flavour is built around the same thing: real fermented dill brine, 180mg of caffeine and a lot of carbonation. Here's the lineup from "easy first sip" to "why did I do this".

4. Dill Candy: the gateway can

Badge: Sweet Fizz · Price: $3.99

Candied pickle syrup with a fizzy rush. It tastes like a carnival pickle-on-a-stick turned into soda: sweet first, tart second, with the dill sitting quietly in the background. If you're scared of the whole concept, start here.

3. Classic Dill: the original shock

Badge: Original · Price: $3.99

The one that started it all. Crisp cucumber, a salty-sweet brine punch and a clean finish. This is the flavour most people mean when they say "the pickle energy drink". It's balanced, it's loud, and it's the best match for salty snacks.

2. Sweet Heat: honey, habanero, garlic

Badge: Habanero · Price: $3.99

Habanero honey and garlic brine in a black can with flames on it, which should tell you everything. The first half of the sip is sweet. The habanero shows up at the very end and stays for a while. Fans of hot honey, this is yours.

1. Sour Blast: maximum pucker

Badge: Pucker x3 · Price: $3.99

Triple-fermented sour pickle with electric lime. It's the flavour behind most of the faces you've seen in #DillDChallenge videos. Try to finish a can without making the face. You won't.

Bonus: the Mystery Can

Every month we drop one limited flavour that's gone for good once it sells out. Right now it's Wasabi Brine Fizz at $4.50, only 5,000 cases. Read how the Mystery Can Drop works or jump straight to the drop countdown.

Which one should you try first?

  • Never had pickle juice? Dill Candy.
  • Love pickles already? Classic Dill.
  • Put hot sauce on everything? Sweet Heat.
  • Want a story for your group chat? Sour Blast.

Can't decide? Build a mixed 12-pack in the shop: mix and match any flavours, and every pack ships with a free sticker sheet.
