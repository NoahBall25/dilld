The #DillDChallenge: inside TikTok's sourest dare

By The DILL'D Lab · October 14, 2026 · 4 min read

On this page

Where it started Our favourite reactions How to do it (the right way) Keep it safe

The rules are simple: crack a cold can of Sour Blast, film yourself, and try to get through it without making the face. Almost nobody makes it.

Where it started

The first videos were just people reacting to the idea of a pickle energy drink. Then the Sour Blast clips took over: the squint, the jaw tighten, the slow "wait… that's actually good". The tag #DillDChallenge stuck, and fans started tagging friends to try it next.

Our favourite reactions

  • @picklequeen went in expecting a joke and came out "hooked" on Classic Dill.
  • @streamer_kai cracked a can before ranked games, live on stream.
  • @snackhunting took Sweet Heat to the streets and felt the habanero at the very end.
  • @brineboyz failed the Sour Blast no-face challenge in under two seconds.

See them all in the TikTok section on the homepage, and follow @drinkdilld for new ones every week.

How to do it (the right way)

  1. Grab one cold can of Sour Blast.
  2. Set up your camera so we can see your whole face. The face is the point.
  3. Sip, don't chug. The challenge is about your reaction, not speed.
  4. Post it with #DillDChallenge and tag @drinkdilld.

Keep it safe

Each can has 180mg of caffeine. That's one can per challenge, full stop. Don't stack cans for views, don't mix with alcohol, and if you're under 18, pregnant or sensitive to caffeine, this one isn't for you. Read how much caffeine is in DILL'D for the full breakdown.
